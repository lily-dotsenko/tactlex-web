# ADR 0004: Human-reviewed publication workflow

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-21

## Decision

All imports and machine-assisted content enter as `DRAFT`. Only an ADMIN may advance terms sequentially to `APPROVED` and `PUBLISHED`, and publication requires complete bilingual fields plus a verified source.

## Why

The domain is safety-sensitive and translation errors can change meaning. Shipping two hundred guessed terms would conflict with the content-integrity requirement.

## Consequences

The technical MVP supports the full 200-term curriculum but starts with a small, visibly draft demonstration set. Completing the editorial catalog is a subject-matter-expert milestone.
